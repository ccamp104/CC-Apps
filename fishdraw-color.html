Fishdraw

Type a seed and press Draw.

Line drawing is the same as . The colour is added by this page.

Primitive

Rebuild this fish out of simple shapes. Each shape is placed where it most improves the likeness, as in Michael Fogleman's primitive.

Lighting

Gives the flat image depth and lights it. Move your mouse or finger over the picture to move the light.

Drawings by fishdraw by Lingdong Huang (MIT licence), running unmodified. Lighting uses a normal map made from the fish's own outlines and lines, in the style of tools like Laigter. Primitive step ported to JavaScript from primitive by Michael Fogleman (MIT licence). The caption font only has letters, so digits and symbols in a seed change the fish but don't appear in the caption.
